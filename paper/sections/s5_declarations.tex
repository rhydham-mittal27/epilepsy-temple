\section*{Declarations}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Declarations}

\paragraph{Data availability.} Both datasets are publicly available; neither is redistributed here.
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{CHB-MIT Scalp EEG Database} \cite{shoeb2009,goldberger2000}: available on PhysioNet under the Open Data Commons Attribution License v1.0. A 3-minute excerpt of \code{chb01_03.edf} is embedded, with attribution, in the interactive model.
\item \textbf{Siena Scalp EEG Database} \cite{detti2020,goldberger2000}: available on PhysioNet (doi:10.13026/5d4a-j060) under CC BY 4.0; streamed, not stored.
\item \textbf{SeizeIT2} \cite{bhagubai2025}: available on OpenNeuro (ds005873 v1.1.0; \url{https://doi.org/10.18112/openneuro.ds005873.v1.1.0}) under CC0.
\end{itemize}
Extracted features, caches and model outputs can be regenerated with the commands in Appendix~\ref{app:record}.

\paragraph{Code availability.} All code (simulators, inference, detection pipelines, analyses, interactive model) and this manuscript's source are publicly available in the repository \url{https://github.com/rhydham-mittal27/epilepsy-temple}.

\paragraph{Ethics.} This study used only public, de-identified datasets collected under the approvals of the original studies; no new human or animal data were collected. The systems described are not medical devices.

\paragraph{Author contributions.} R.M.\ contributed all of the following:
\begin{itemize}
\item conceived the study and the ear-hook concept;
\item directed all experiments;
\item interpreted the results;
\item wrote the manuscript.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Use of AI tools.} The author used an AI assistant (Claude, Anthropic) throughout the project to speed up literature search, code implementation, running experiments, analysis and drafting of text. All design decisions, including the choice of datasets and evaluation metrics, were the author's, and the author reviewed every output and takes full responsibility for the content. In line with common publication policy, the AI tool is not listed as an author.

\paragraph{Competing interests.} None. This work is independent of, and not affiliated with, Temple Wearables or Continue Research.

\paragraph{Funding.} None.
